Common Questions

Do you supply builders without installing?
Yes. Supply only is a standard part of what we do — we fabricate to your schedule and you handle installation with your own trades.
How do I get pricing on a project?
Send us your plans or window schedule and we'll price from them. If you don't have a schedule yet, we can work from architectural drawings or a set of measurements.
What are your lead times for a full house package?
Production is typically completed within three to four weeks of the order being confirmed. For programmed work, talk to us early and we'll confirm dates against your build schedule.
Can you supply thermally broken systems for 7-star homes?
Yes, the AluBREAK-COMM range covers thermally broken windows, sliding and stacker doors and hinge doors. Contact us for the specific performance data your energy assessor needs.
Do you deliver to site?
Contact us to discuss delivery arrangements for your project.
Where are the products made?
At our own factory in Sunbury, in Melbourne's north-west. That's also why our turnaround is measured in weeks rather than months.
