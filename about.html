About DH Aluminium Windows & Doors

Where Precision Meets Design

DH Aluminium is a Melbourne aluminium windows and doors company committed to delivering systems that combine architectural beauty with structural excellence. We work from our Sunbury base across metropolitan Melbourne and the north-west growth corridor.

Premium Materials — Every system uses high-grade aluminium profiles engineered for durability and long-term performance.
Custom Solutions — We work closely with architects, builders, and homeowners to deliver tailored systems for every project.
End-to-End Service — From design consultation through to installation, we manage every step with care and precision.
Quality Assured — All products are tested to the highest standards, ensuring safety, performance, and longevity.
