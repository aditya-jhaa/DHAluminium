Aluminium Windows

Double Hung & Sashless Windows Melbourne

Both styles slide vertically rather than sideways. Double hung windows use two sashes in a traditional arrangement; sashless designs remove the meeting frame altogether for a cleaner run of glass.

Double hung suits you if…

  • You're matching a period, heritage or traditional look
  • You want ventilation from the top and bottom of the opening
  • The window faces a walkway where nothing should project
  • You're replacing existing double hung windows

Sashless suits you if…

  • You want the least visible framing and the most glass
  • Easy cleaning from inside matters — sashes lift out
  • You want a clean contemporary look over a traditional one
  • The window overlooks a view worth keeping unbroken

Common Questions

What does sashless actually mean?
In a sashless window the glass panels sit directly in the frame without individual surrounding sash frames, and there's no central meeting rail. The result is more glass and less aluminium in your line of sight.
Are double hung windows harder to operate?
Modern aluminium double hung windows are counterbalanced, so the sashes stay where you put them and move with light pressure. They're very different from old timber double hungs with cords and weights.
Can sashless windows be double glazed?
Yes, though the frame requirements differ from single glazing. We'll confirm what's achievable for your specific opening size when we quote.
How long will it take?
Production is typically completed within three to four weeks of your order being confirmed. Installation is usually finished in a single day, scheduled around your availability.
Can I arrange my own installation?
Yes. We offer supply only as well as supply and installation, so you can use your own trades if you prefer. Builders regularly order supply only from us.
What colours are available?
Everything is finished in powder coat, with a wide standard colour range plus custom colours on request. Charcoal, black and monument are the most common choices we install.
